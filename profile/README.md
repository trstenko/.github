# Trstenko

Trstenko is a small assistant that sits on your desk and wakes up when you say its name. We're a student company from Maribor, Slovenia, and the device is still in development.

## Help us teach it its name

To recognise "Trstenko", the device has to hear lots of different people say it. Recording a few takes on the voice collector takes about two minutes and helps a lot:

**[voice-collector.trstenko.com](https://voice-collector.trstenko.com)**
